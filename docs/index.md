features:
  - title: A model compiles, code runs
    details: A local model builds each tool once. Every call after that is plain code, typically 1 to 5 seconds.
  - title: Repairs itself
    details: When a site changes, toolDrop fixes only the failing step, tests the fix and publishes a new version while your call waits.
  - title: Free and local
    details: Runs on free local models through Ollama. No paid API, no paid service, and nothing leaves your machine.
  - title: Careful with your accounts
    details: An encrypted vault, credentials masked from every prompt, and an approval before any write.
